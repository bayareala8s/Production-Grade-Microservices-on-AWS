# Capstone — Grading Rubric (Suggested)

## Total: 100 points

### Architecture & contracts (25)

- 10: Clear service/data ownership boundaries
- 10: API/event contracts are explicit and versioned/backward compatible
- 5: Design decisions documented (ADRs)

### Implementation quality (25)

- 10: Critical flow works end-to-end reliably
- 10: Handles failure modes (timeouts/retries/DLQ/compensations)
- 5: Code structure supports maintainability

### Security (15)

- 10: Least privilege approach documented (IAM roles / permissions)
- 5: Secrets are handled safely (no hardcoding)

### Observability & operability (20)

- 10: Logs/metrics/traces are usable for debugging
- 5: Health checks and dashboards exist
- 5: Runbooks are practical and complete

### Delivery & communication (15)

- 10: CI/CD pipeline exists and enforces quality
- 5: Demo is clear and explains tradeoffs and incidents
