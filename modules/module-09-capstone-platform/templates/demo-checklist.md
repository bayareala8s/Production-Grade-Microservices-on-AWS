# Demo Checklist (10–15 minutes)

- architecture overview (1–2 min)
- show service boundaries and data ownership (1 min)
- walk a critical flow end-to-end (3–5 min)
- show observability: dashboard + request correlation (2–3 min)
- demonstrate a failure and mitigation/rollback (2–3 min)
- wrap-up: tradeoffs and what you’d do next (1–2 min)
