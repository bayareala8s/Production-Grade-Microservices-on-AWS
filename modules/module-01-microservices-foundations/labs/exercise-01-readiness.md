# Exercise 01 — Microservices Readiness Checklist

## Outcome

Students can articulate whether microservices are a fit and what must be true organizationally and operationally for success.

## Instructions (20–30 min)

Pick a product scenario (or use your current workplace app). Answer:

1. What is the strongest reason microservices would help?
2. What is the strongest reason microservices would hurt?
3. Who owns on-call? How will incidents be handled?
4. Do you have CI/CD maturity for frequent deploys?
5. Do you have observability practices (logs/metrics/traces)?

## “Microservices are a good fit when…”

- multiple teams need autonomous delivery
- parts of the system have different scaling needs
- clear domain boundaries exist
- you can invest in platform/ops maturity

## “Microservices are risky when…”

- unclear ownership (no on-call, no product alignment)
- shared database and tight coupling are unavoidable
- you cannot afford distributed systems complexity yet

## Deliverable

One page summary with:

- yes/no decision
- the top 3 risks and mitigations
